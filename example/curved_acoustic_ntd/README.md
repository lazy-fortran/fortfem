# Curved acoustic Neumann-to-Dirichlet map

This example maps the normal displacement of an arbitrary closed polygonal
interface to its outgoing acoustic pressure. It uses the P1/P0 Helmholtz
Calderón operators and a resonance-safe Burton--Miller combination rather than
an enclosing planar or circular artificial boundary.

For the `exp(-i omega t)` convention and the solid outward normal,

\[
  \partial_n p = \rho_f \omega^2 u_n.
\]

The numerical solution on polygonal circles is compared with the independent
outgoing Hankel eigenmode

\[
  p_m = \rho_f\omega^2
        \frac{H_m^{(1)}(kR)}
             {k {H_m^{(1)}}'(kR)} u_m.
\]

The interface condition follows the pressure/displacement formulation used by
the acoustic-paper fixture at revision `6300ab0`. The combined integral
equation follows Burton and Miller, *Proceedings of the Royal Society A* 323
(1971), 201--210, DOI `10.1098/rspa.1971.0097`.

The example also runs the monolithic complex P1 elasticity solve on a disk.
An exact rigid translation is loaded by its analytical inertia and outgoing
Hankel pressure traction. The displacement error therefore verifies the full
chain from structural normal trace through BEM pressure to consistent
structural boundary work.

Generated images are written under `output/example/curved_acoustic_ntd/`;
images are generated by CI and are not stored in git.
