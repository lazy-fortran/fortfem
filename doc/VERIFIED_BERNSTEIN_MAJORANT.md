# Verified Bernstein audit of an RT_k equilibrated flux

`fortfem_verified_bernstein_majorant` audits the sampled RT_k flux (k = 1, 2, 3)
of `equilibrated_flux_estimate` on a conforming, nonoverlapping,
counter-clockwise affine triangle mesh. It supports `-div(A grad u) = f` with
`A = 1/w`, `w > 0` affine on every cell (vertex values), `f = s/w^m`
(`m = 0, 1, 2`) with `s` a polynomial given by outward enclosures of its
Bernstein coefficients, homogeneous Dirichlet data on the whole boundary and a
conforming affine P1/P2/P3 candidate `u_h`.

The audited field is not the floating RT_k field. On every cell the RT_k field
is sampled at the degree-(k+1) lattice and converted to Bernstein coefficients
in floating point. Each global edge then selects, at its first cell, one
exactly represented normal component per Bernstein position. The mathematical
field replaces the normal components of every cell's edge coefficients by these
shared values; at a corner, the two edge normals fix the coefficient. The
restriction of a Bernstein polynomial to an edge depends only on that edge's
coefficients, so neighbouring normal traces are identical and the cellwise
BDM_(k+1) field lies in H(div). Fitting and rounding only change which H(div)
field is audited, so they can loosen but never invalidate the bound.

For this field and `C_F` with `||v|| <= C_F ||v||_A` on H1_0,

```
||u-u_h||_A <= ||A^(-1/2)(sigma-A grad u_h)|| + C_F ||f+div sigma||.
```

With `q = w sigma - grad u_h` and `t = s + w^m div sigma`, both polynomials,
`int |q|^2/w <= int |q|^2 l_1` and `int t^2/w^(2m) <= int t^2 l_(2m)`, where
`l_j` is the chord of the convex `w^-j` between the cell minimum and maximum
of `w`. The chord is affine and lies above `w^-j`, so its relative excess is
`O((h/w)^2)`. All polynomial integrals use exact Bernstein moments in outward
interval arithmetic, so quadrature and arithmetic error are enclosed. There is
no sampled quadrature, exactness assumption, projection or local-solve
accuracy requirement. Every leaf is generated by
`tools/codegen/app/gen_verified_bernstein_products.f90`, which proves the
product, elevation, derivative, P2/P3-conversion and integral identities natively
before emission.

The residual uses the global `C_F`, not the elementwise Payne--Weinberger
oscillation of the sampled estimate. For a smooth source `||f+div sigma||` is
`O(h^(k+1))` against an `O(h^k)` energy error, so the certified radius
approaches the sampled one under refinement. Curved cells, Neumann or nonzero
Dirichlet data, discontinuous candidates, unchecked source numerators and
pointwise bands are outside this audit. FortNum's strict IEEE execution
contract applies.

For non-polynomial `s`, the optional `source_remainder(cell, radius, ok)`
callback supplies a caller-proved whole-cell bound `|s-p| <= radius%hi`, with
`p` supplied through the existing Bernstein source callback. Coefficient
intervals enclose one polynomial approximation; sampled errors do not meet
this contract. If `P_T` encloses the polynomial residual integral, Minkowski
and `w >= w_min > 0` give

```
int_T |f+div sigma|^2 <= (sqrt(P_T) + rho_T sqrt(|T|)/w_min^m)^2.
```

The generated tail leaf evaluates this expression outwardly. Its residual
interval starts at zero because the remainder can cancel the polynomial
residual. Invalid, negative, non-finite or unavailable remainder data fail
with `VERIFIED_BERNSTEIN_BAD_SOURCE`; overflow also fails. No approximation
is silently accepted as an exact source. This is a fixed-source theorem,
not a nonlinear stability theorem. Cell callbacks and accumulation remain
OpenMP-parallel with O(N) work/storage for fixed degrees. P3 conversion is
proved at all ten Lagrange nodes before emission.

`test_verified_bernstein_majorant` checks the bound above independent
Duffy-quadrature energy errors for P1/P2 interpolants with `A = 1/(1+x)`,
exact reproduction of the sampled flux defect and divergence defect for
`A = 1` and `f` in `P_k`, shared-edge repair of inconsistent coefficients,
exact-zero containment, and rejection of invalid sources, weights and degrees.

The source-tail tests cover exact zero recovery, all three weight powers
against the exact norm of a constant remainder, and rejection of invalid
callbacks. KIN6D's `grad_shafranov_checked_source_remainder` independently
solves a quartic manufactured state with a rational numerator, bounds its
Taylor remainder by a geometric-series identity, and checks containment,
efficiency and rate on every P1/P2/P3 mesh. Its separate `slow` study retains
repeated 1/2/4-thread timings. Mapped cells and Neumann traces still require
separate geometry and boundary enclosures.
