# Meridian sheet interpolation remainder

`meridian_hcurl_edge_remainder` bounds the energy correction caused by
replacing the interpolated density `I_k G` with `G`. It uses the gauge
`R A_phi = 0`, mode `n != 0`, and the `R dR dZ` pairing of
`meridian_hcurl_equilibrated_bound`. The metric may be the positive toroidal
radius or the constant periodic-cylinder radius.

For an oriented edge of a counterclockwise triangle, let `r = G - I_k G`.
The equispaced interpolant includes both endpoints. Assuming `G` is H1 on
the edge, `r` vanishes at its endpoints. The lift

```
s = lambda_a + lambda_b,  tau = lambda_b/s,
sigma = -s^4 r(tau)
```

belongs to H1 of the listed cell, vanishes on its other two edges, and
jumps by `-r` when extended by zero. The fourth power confines the
oscillatory interpolation defect near the loaded edge. Sum the lifts in
each cell before taking the norm, including cells carrying several loads.
With `H = (grad(sigma)/(i n), sigma/R)`, integration by parts gives
`(curl_n v,H)_R = L_r(v)`. Therefore, for the correction `e` with the same
volume current and homogeneous boundary data,

```
|||e|||^2 = L_r(e) <= ||curl_n e||_R ||H||_R <= |||e||| ||H||_R.
```

The constant is one, including any nonnegative exterior energy term.
Adding this radius to the interpolated-load reconstruction radius is a
triangle inequality. Each physical loaded edge must be listed once.
The callbacks return `G` and `dG/dtau`, must agree at interpolation nodes,
and must be thread-safe. A boundary sheet can be treated the same way.
Vertex mismatches in a separately assembled broken lift need their own
accounting; this edge-interior correction does not repair them.

The bound is evaluated by sampled volume quadrature, with ordinary
floating-point arithmetic. It is a **practical** majorant, not a numerical
certificate. It does not estimate an approximate exterior operator or
certify sensitivities. Local coordinate/lift primal, JVP and VJP routines
are generated by `gen_meridian_hcurl_equilibration_products` at the existing
`fortsym.lock`. Edge interpolation uses FortNum's Lagrange routines. The
hot loop allocates no scratch and is parallel over the compact loaded-cell
list; setup storage/work is O(N), and quadrature work is linear in the
number of loaded cells at fixed order.

`test_meridian_edge_remainder` checks cubic recovery to rounding, mode
parity, generated JVP/VJP consistency, and a cosine sheet in the cylinder
rectangle `[0,1] x [-1/2,1/2]`. The continuous correction is independent:
the cosine coefficients of `G-I_3 G` are integrated analytically and its
energy is summed through 16384 modes, with a doubled-cutoff check. On
2, 4 and 8 sheet segments the measured index is 2.23, 2.35 and 2.39;
the observed orders are 3.47 and 3.49. The series is a numerical reference,
not an interval enclosure of its tail. Runtime checks and cost/scaling
measurements are retained by the KIN6D consumer's named test gate.
